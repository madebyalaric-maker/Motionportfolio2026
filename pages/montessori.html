AD for Entri Montessori TTC

A cinematic advertisement crafted using generative AI, combining storytelling, visual innovation, and refined post-production for Entri Montessori TTC.

Breakdown

Tools Used

Magnific - Spaces | Comfy UI | Davinici Resolve

Overview

For this project, the goal was to create a cinematic and emotionally engaging advertisement for Entri Montessori TTC using generative AI while avoiding the typical "AI slop" look often seen in AI-generated videos. Every shot was crafted to feel natural, believable, and visually grounded, with a strong focus on realism and storytelling.

To achieve this, I experimented with multiple AI models throughout the production process, combining their strengths to build a cohesive visual narrative. It was an exciting project that allowed me to explore the creative potential of AI while maintaining the quality and emotion of a premium commercial film.
