Motion Design with a Stop-Motion Aesthetic

A mixed-media advertisement blending digital stop-motion, motion design, and creative storytelling for Entri's Digital Marketing Course.

Breakdown

Tools Used

After Effects | Premiere Pro | Photoshop

Overview

This project explored a mixed-media visual style inspired by digital stop-motion, combining motion design, compositing, and creative storytelling for Entri’s Digital Marketing Course. The goal was to create a visually engaging piece that felt handcrafted, energetic, and memorable while communicating the course in a fresh and unconventional way.

Using a combination of design, animation, and post-production techniques, I crafted each scene to balance playful visuals with clear storytelling. It was an exciting project that allowed me to experiment with different creative approaches and bring together multiple disciplines into a cohesive final film.
