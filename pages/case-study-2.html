Case Study · Product Design Concept

Healthcare Made Simple

Introducing Healr

A doctor appointment booking app that bridges the gap between patients and healthcare providers enabling seamless online consultations and in-clinic visit scheduling.

ROLE UI/UX Designer
PLATFORM Android & iOS
TOOLS Figma, Claude Code, Visual Studio Code

About Healr

Healr is a concept mobile application designed to simplify how people access healthcare — connecting patients with both online (teleconsultation) and offline (in-clinic) doctors. Users can filter by specialty, view real-time availability, and pay seamlessly within the app.

First Impressions Matter

The onboarding distinguishes between new and existing users, personalizing the welcome experience. New users complete a guided profile setup that collects essential health information.

Everything You Need for Better Healthcare

Three core pillars power the Healr experience connecting patients with doctors online, in-person, and keeping their health records organized.

Online Consultation

Connect with verified doctors via Teleconsultation instantly or by scheduling a video call. Filter by specialty, language, and availability in real-time.

In-Clinic Visits

Book physical appointments with doctors nearby. View clinic location on map, morning and afternoon slots, and consultation fees — all upfront.

Medical Records

Upload and access medical history, prescriptions, and test reports at any time.

What I Learned

Designing Healr reinforced how important clarity and trust are in healthcare UX. Every decision from the slot picker to the payment confirmation was made with the user's peace of mind at the centre.

Key Insights

  • Clear information builds trust, visibility of pricing, availability, and consultation types reduces hesitation.
  • Early interactions set expectations, thoughtful onboarding helps users quickly understand how the system works.
  • Small usability improvements can significantly impact decision speed and overall experience.
